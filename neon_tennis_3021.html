🎮 HOW TO PLAY

• Paddle Movement: Move mouse cursor up and down, or slide your finger on mobile touchscreen. You can also press [W/S] or [▲/▼] arrow keys.

• Edge Slice & Spin: Hitting the ball with the outer corners of your paddle imparts aggressive deflection angles and speed bursts.

• Pause Control: Press PAUSE at any time or hit [P] on your keyboard.

• Victory: Reach the match target score before the AI to win the round!

🪙 COIN EARNING SYSTEM TUTORIAL
